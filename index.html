Roguelike Deckbuilder

MENON：夢 / MENON: The Dream

可以擊倒，也可以放過。Strike them down, or let them go.

青銅時代的夢境卡牌肉鴿。整副牌都在手上，出掉的牌要靠記憶、墓園或神的祝福才拿得回來；每一場戰鬥，你都能選擇擊倒敵人或放過他們。改編自創作中的小說《米農》。 A Bronze Age dream roguelike deckbuilder. Your whole deck starts in your hand, and played cards only return through memory, the graveyard or a god's blessing. In every fight you choose: strike your enemy down, or let them go. Adapted from the novel MENON, a work in progress.

關於我們About

一個人，一盞燈One maker, one lamp

✦

Keelu 目前只有一個人：寫小說，也寫程式，每天用零碎的時間把故事做成可以玩的東西。 Keelu is one person for now: a novelist who also writes code, turning stories into something you can play, a little every day.

故事先行Story first

每款遊戲都從一個故事開始。《MENON：夢》跟著小說《米農》一起長大。Every game starts from a story. MENON: The Dream grows alongside the novel it comes from.

選擇有重量Choices that weigh

我們喜歡讓玩家停下來想一想的設計：這張牌要不要打、這個人要不要放過。We like designs that make you pause: play this card or keep it, strike or spare.

持續點燈Keep the light on

遊戲上線後持續更新，並公開更新紀錄，聽玩家的聲音調整。Games keep growing after launch, with public update logs shaped by player feedback.
