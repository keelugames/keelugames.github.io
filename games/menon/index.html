MENON：夢MENON: The Dream

可以擊倒，也可以放過。Strike them down, or let them go.

你是補網的少年 Seyagales，還不知道自己會成為傳說。在夢裡，你帶著一整副牌走過戰場、商店、墓園與神殿——而夢會記得你的每一個選擇。 You are Seyagales, a boy who mends fishing nets and does not yet know he will become a legend. In the dream you carry a whole deck through battlefields, markets, graveyards and temples — and the dream remembers every choice you make.

夢的規則Rules of the dream

牌是補給，不是彈藥Your deck is your supply line

✦

每位角色都有自己的牌組容量：帶一星牌能帶很多張，帶高星牌只能帶幾張。整副牌要撐完整場夢，走錯一步，就得到墓園去把牌喚醒。Each character has a deck capacity: one-star cards let you bring many, high-star cards only a few. The deck must last the whole dream — misstep, and you'll be visiting the graveyard to wake your cards back up.

擊倒Strike拿到銀幣、一張新牌，再挑一個強化。Gain silver, a new card and an upgrade.
放過Let go多取回 3 張用掉的牌，還有機會招募對方。被你放過的人，之後可能回來報恩——或報仇。Recover 3 extra spent cards, with a chance to recruit them. Those you spare may return later — to repay you, or not.

原作小說The novel

《米農》MENON

✦

遊戲改編自創作中的小說《米農》。一個只想過平靜日子的海邊少年被帶進戰爭，慢慢變成別人口中的傳說。The game is adapted from MENON, a novel in progress. A coastal boy who only wants quiet days is taken to war, and slowly becomes the legend other people tell.

Seyagales 是他自己；Menon，是戰爭之後別人口中的他。Seyagales is who he is. Menon is who others say he became.

遊戲是他的夢：小說寫到哪裡，夢裡就會多出哪些人。小說將陸續公開，遊戲跟著故事一起更新。The game is his dream: as the novel grows, new people walk into the dream. The novel will be released over time, and the game grows with it.
